The Big Question

When a neighborhood adds new businesses and homes, do the people who live there also benefit?

I used the Inclusive Growth Score to compare growth (is the area getting bigger?) with inclusion (is everyone sharing in it?).

The Short Answer

Growth is ahead of inclusion in all four neighborhoods I studied.

Neighborhood (Parish)	Overall Score	Inclusion	Growth	Gap
Bossier	56	45	66	+21
Union	54	49	60	+11
Bienville	32	27	37	+10
Ascension*	28	26	29	+3
